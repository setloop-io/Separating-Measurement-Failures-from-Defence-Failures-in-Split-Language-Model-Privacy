### 2.1 PB-R1 (`review-llm-attack-suite--a-unified-red-team-framework-for-privacy-evaluation-of-split-and-distributed-llm-systems.pdf` — XSci / PoPETs-style)

- **PB-R1 Major 1 (Token Gate Statistical Validity, $z=2.5392$, Token Correlation & Sample-Size Floors):**
  - *Finding:* The token gate uses an unclustered binomial Wilson bound with $z=2.5392$ (derived from a 9-probe known-plaintext setting), ignores intra-document token correlation, and produces finite-sample floors above $1.00\text{ pp}$ at $n=768$ (and on high-baseline corpora at $n=3{,}072$), making a $\le 1.00\text{ pp}$ pass unreachable in those unclustered settings.
  - *Remediation (Fix 2):* Updated Section 3 (`main.tex`), Section 5.2 (`Table 2`), and Supplementary Section S3.2 (`Tables S4, S5, S6`) to (i) give the exact sample-size thresholds $n_a$ required for `floor_pp(p0, n_a) <= 1.00 pp` ($n_a \ge 639$ at $p_0=0\%$, $2{,}337$ at $p_0=2.64\%$, $3{,}534$ at $p_0=4.69\%$, $4{,}411$ at $p_0=6.25\%$, and $10{,}884$ at $p_0=20.31\%$), (ii) explain explicitly that when the finite-sample binomial floor exceeds $1.00\text{ pp}$, the unclustered Wilson bound serves only as a large-leakage screen and cannot certify a $\le 1.00\text{ pp}$ pass without larger $n_a$ or the paired document-clustered interval, and (iii) report the paired point excesses ($\hat p_a - p_0$) and document-clustered 95% bootstrap intervals (`clustered-gate-v1`) directly in `Table 2`, `Table S4`, `Table S5`, and `Table S6`.

- **PB-R1 Major 2 (Separation of Shared Companion Evidence vs. New Contributions & Comparison with Related Frameworks):**
  - *Finding:* The paper shares its flagship 3,680-run GPT-2 experiment (`v3`) with `politis2026layercount` and references the Qwen audit from `politis2026privacyfailure`; the boundary between shared evidence and new contributions, as well as the feature comparison against `VFLAIR-LLM`, `garak`, `ML Privacy Meter`, and `LLM-PBE`, should be explicit.
  - *Remediation (Fix 1):* Updated Section 1 (`main.tex`) to enumerate the two shared studies alongside the empirical studies new to Paper B, and added `Table 4` (`\ref{tab:framework-comparison}`) in Section 7 comparing `VFLAIR-LLM`, `garak`, `ML Privacy Meter`, `LLM-PBE`, and `LLM-Attack Suite` across five evaluation-protocol capabilities.

- **PB-R1 Major 3 (35 of 64 Modules Qualified Only on Synthetic Fixtures / Simulators):**
  - *Finding:* Over half of the 64 registered modules (`35/64`) carry `Q-SYN` synthetic/simulator qualification only, and the main campaign's reconstruction script is separate from the registered CLI module.
  - *Remediation (Fix 1 & Fix 3):* Section 1 and Section 6 (`main.tex`) explicitly state the `Q-REAL` / `PARTIAL` / `Q-SYN` / `HELPER` breakdown and that synthetic qualification tests contract mechanics rather than real-model attack effectiveness. Sections 2.4, 5.4, and Supplementary Section S2 clarify that both `attacker/reconstruction_baselines.py` and `attacker/attacks/seam_inversion.py` reside in the `attacker` package and share the capture format, `SplitPlan` head interface, and `EvidenceLog`, differing only in whether the joint arm takes $k/2+k/2$ or all $k$ candidates at $t\ge 1$ from the backward gradient score.

- **PB-R1 Major 4 (Empirical Scope Centered on GPT-2 124M and Short 32–128 Token Windows):**
  - *Finding:* Quantitative text reconstruction is concentrated on GPT-2 124M (plus Mistral-7B SipIt qualification), with Qwen models used for structural/key-solving/agentic qualification rather than full joint gradient-plus-activation text reconstruction on modern GQA/SwiGLU architectures.
  - *Remediation (Fix 8):* Executed two major post-review expansions: (i) a **150-cell longer-passage joint evaluation** across all 255 held-out WikiText-2 articles with $\ge 128$ tokens at $T\in\{32,64,128\}$ (`+3.17`, `+2.91`, and `+3.02 pp` token gains for `joint_true_k32` over `forward_k32`, and `2.75x`, `4.96x`, and `23.5x` exact-passage gains), and (ii) a **304-cell controlled Qwen3-0.6B (28 layers, $d=1024$, $V=151{,}936$, GQA, RoPE, QK-Norm, SwiGLU, RMSNorm) joint gradient-plus-activation text-reconstruction benchmark** across split depths $L\in\{1,7,14,21\}$ and grants `{public, exact}` on 8 victim seeds (`11..88`) and 256 held-out 32-token passages (`output/paper-b-qwen3-recon-20261020/`), showing that at midpoint $L=14$ (`public`), `joint_true_k32` (`69.05%`) outperforms `forward_k32` (`55.20%`) by `+13.85 pp` (95% CI `[+12.38, +15.27] pp`) and `forward_k128` (`57.18%`) by `+11.88 pp` at one-fourth the candidate budget (`joint_true_k128 = 71.35%`), while with trained layers `joint_true_k32` reaches `86.87%` (`+14.08 pp`, exact docs `5.27%`) at $L=14$ and `96.85%` (`+21.37 pp`, exact docs `44.58%`) at $L=21$.
