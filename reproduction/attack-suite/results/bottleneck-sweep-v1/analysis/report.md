## Scope and limitations

- development sweep for method/dim selection; not a confirmatory security claim
- three victim seeds; intervals are conditional on this fixed article sample
- trained defenders are in-repo re-implementations, not the original defense projects' code or weights
- the defense-aware surrogate is a PCA stand-in for the true defense map; it bounds a class-and-calibration adversary, not a defense-internals adversary
- the MINE reading in bottleneck provenance is a diagnostic, never a certificate
- split depth, dim and method are confounded with cell ordering on a single host
